# Experimentation and incrementality

## Interpretation

Attribution assigns credit; incrementality estimates what would have happened without the treatment. Randomization, interference, lag and selection bias determine whether a comparison is causal. Sample requirements depend on baseline variance and minimum detectable effect. Repeated peeking, many variants and changing stopping rules can create false discoveries.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
