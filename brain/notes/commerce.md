# Commerce and feeds

## Interpretation

A feed is a changing source of product truth. Inventory, price, destination, freshness and campaign selection must agree. An accepted upload does not prove every product is eligible or every ad is delivering. Product updates need stable identifiers and diagnostics rather than duplicate catalog creation.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
