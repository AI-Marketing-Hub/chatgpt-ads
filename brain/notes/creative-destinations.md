# Creative and destinations

## Interpretation

An ad and destination make one promise. Strong copy cannot repair an unsupported price, inaccessible offer or mismatched landing experience. Rendering limits, recommended lengths and maximum lengths are different constraints. Visible validation proves a particular version at a particular time.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
