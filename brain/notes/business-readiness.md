# Business readiness

## Interpretation

A usable advertising brief connects an approved offer to a measurable business outcome. Revenue, margin, lead value and retention assumptions belong to the client. Missing economics allow exploratory research but prevent a defensible profitability target. Approved claims and destinations are inputs, not facts an agent should invent.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
