# Policy and privacy

## Interpretation

Platform eligibility, legal obligations and account approval are separate determinations. A previous approval does not exempt a changed destination or data flow. Data purpose, audience, retention and deletion ownership must be specified before persistence. Hashing an identifier does not make it anonymous. Jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions require current source research and appropriate human judgment.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
