# Strategy and economics

## Interpretation

An auction bid, budget and profitability target serve different purposes. Scenario forecasts are conditional calculations, not expected platform performance. Break-even acquisition cost depends on contribution after variable costs; revenue ROAS alone does not establish profit. Changes to optimization objectives can change which outcomes are comparable.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
