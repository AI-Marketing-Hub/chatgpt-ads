# Targeting and delivery

## Interpretation

Conversation relevance and audience membership are distinct signals. A documented control does not reveal the ranking formula. Broad defaults and omitted fields can expand delivery. Audience eligibility, consent and match quality are separate questions; access to a file is not permission to upload it.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
