# Reliability and maintenance

## Interpretation

A documentation update, account capability change and runtime failure are different kinds of drift. Lessons should change a future decision and carry their evidence scope. One account observation cannot establish a universal benchmark. Historical evidence is retained with supersession links; it must not remain silently authoritative after a contradiction.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
