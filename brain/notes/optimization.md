# Optimization and diagnosis

## Interpretation

Diagnosis should identify the failing stage before prescribing a change: eligibility, delivery, engagement, destination conversion, attribution or economics. A short-term fluctuation is not necessarily a causal response. Preserve a baseline and choose a measurable decision window. An optimization proposal needs a downside and a reversal condition.

This interpretation is methodological guidance, not an additional assertion about current platform availability.
