## policy-eligibility

Early access focuses on household and consumer goods, local services, travel and entertainment, and digital products and education. Other categories are disallowed at launch unless specifically allowed. Financial, health, and legal services may be approved case-by-case in the US only, subject to category rules and possible credential proof.

## policy-billing-safety

Billing is postpay. A daily budget is an average over seven days, not a same-day cap, and a day can bill up to twice the daily budget while seven-day media spend stays within seven times that budget. Campaign-total budget is the campaign maximum. Payment threshold determines charge timing, is not an account spend cap, and cannot be manually changed. Pausing can still yield billable delivery for up to 24 hours.

Evidence state: evidence_based. Availability: documented_not_account_verified.

Interpretation: Use campaign-total budgets for hard campaign ceilings, do not equate payment threshold with spend control, retain a pause buffer, and confirm the billing entity, country, currency, payment method, and tax data before activation.

- [Billing & Payment](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001216-billing-payment) (`policy-src-billing`; retrieved 2026-09-10)

## refresh-account-spend-controls-20260911

For ad accounts on postpaid invoice billing, the Advertiser API documents account-wide date-range and daily spending limits shared across campaigns. These limits use the account currency and timezone, do not allocate or pace campaign spend, and apply to billable ad spend rather than taxes or the total invoice. Delivery can take time to stop. The list response distinguishes date-range limits from the daily limit and uses null for unavailable spend counters; null or omission is not zero. All reads and writes in this API section require permission to manage billing.

Evidence state: evidence_based. Availability: documented_postpaid_invoice_and_permission_dependent.

Interpretation: Read and reconcile the current limit configuration before proposing a change. Preserve campaign budgets as separate controls, model stopping delay, and never treat a missing spend counter as zero.

- [Ad Account - Ads](https://developers.openai.com/ads/api-reference/ad-account) (`refresh-ad-account-api-20260911`; retrieved 2026-09-11)
