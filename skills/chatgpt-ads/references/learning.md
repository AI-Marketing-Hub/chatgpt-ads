# Learning and private evidence

Capture a candidate only if it could change a future decision. Record the observation, source evidence, client/account scope, period, hypothesis, limitations and proposed reusable text. Keep the candidate in its authorized private workspace.

Review evidence sufficiency, counterexamples and generalizability. Strip client identifiers, confidential amounts, customer data and identifying combinations before shared promotion. Automated string checks assist review but cannot prove anonymization.

Use operating_core's learning validation with evidence and review records. A policy or authority change also needs a human decision bound to the exact proposal. Preserve rejected and superseded candidates privately with their reasons. The shared brain receives only the reviewed general lesson and public-safe provenance, never the private raw evidence bundle.

The first implementation provides explicit candidate and promotion checks; it does not silently monitor all conversations or upload lessons. Capture is part of the monitor and action closeout workflows. No background scheduling is enabled.
