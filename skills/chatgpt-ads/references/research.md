# Verify and maintain ChatGPT Ads evidence

## Inputs

A question, changed source or coverage gap. Client data is not required for general research.

## Procedure

1. Find the relevant claim IDs and conflicts before searching. Use official OpenAI product, Help, policy and developer sources first. Follow relevant linked collections; record searched and unread coverage separately.

2. Record exact URL, retrieval date, known publication date, section locator, claim scope and evidence class. Partner performance material stays attributed with its sample, period and limitations. Do not turn a case study into a benchmark.

3. Compare the source with the canonical registry. Prepare an incremental proposal; review its affected claims and notes, then apply through the hash-checked knowledge command. Preserve curated note text and history.

4. Reverify changed dependent claims before clearing needs_review. Rebuild generated projections. Return unresolved conflicts and account checks still needed.

## Output

Source-backed answer or reviewed registry proposal, affected workflows and source coverage report.

## Evidence and failure behavior

Use current source IDs from `sources.json` and check `capabilities.md` and conflicts before making platform claims. Missing decisions return `needs_input`; missing evidence returns `no_data`; unavailable tools return `capability_unavailable`; external effects lacking approval return `needs_approval`. Keep partial work and observed failures explicit.

See [knowledge maintenance](../../../docs/KNOWLEDGE.md) for proposal, apply and projection commands.

Use the research section of [workflow output templates](workflow-outputs.md) to structure the deliverable.
