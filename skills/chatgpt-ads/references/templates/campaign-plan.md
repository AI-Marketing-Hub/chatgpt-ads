# Campaign planning worksheet

Status: provisional until the required business and account evidence is supplied.

| Decision | Required evidence | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer | Verified product capabilities, price, proof, landing page | One offer fact sheet |
| Eligibility | Advertiser entity, billing country, target market, category, account state | Documented and account-visible eligibility separately |
| Objective | Business outcome, measurable event, current available objectives | Objective and reason |
| Economics | Margin, fulfillment/refund costs, conversion value, approved loss tolerance | Break-even and target economics with explicit assumptions |
| Budget | Owner ceiling, campaign duration, verified pacing semantics | Scenario range and hard ceiling where supported |
| Ad groups | Distinct customer tasks and offer relevance | Context hypotheses, never guaranteed conversation targeting |
| Creative | Claims and proof, current specs, distinct benefit angles | Draft titles, descriptions, image briefs and destination |
| Measurement | Consent, event map, attribution, timezone, lag, data owner | Measurement protocol and primary outcome |
| Review | Policy evidence and account review | Per-gate readiness and blockers |
| Decision | Sufficient sample, conversion lag, risk, expected value of more evidence | Review window and conditional stop/continue rule |

A forecast is a scenario, not evidence. Report all unknown input cells explicitly. No assumed CPA or ROAS benchmark belongs in this worksheet.
