# Experiment worksheet

Record a falsifiable question, assignment unit, eligible population, treatment, comparator, primary outcome, guardrails, minimum detectable effect, baseline and variance inputs, sample calculation, duration, conversion lag and exclusion rules. Specify one decision rule before observing results.

Confirm whether the needed assignment or holdout mechanism actually exists for this account. A before/after comparison with no control is observational. A vendor claim or platform-attributed lift is not a randomized result.

For the readout, report assignment integrity, exposure, exclusions, missingness, outcome estimates and uncertainty, novelty/seasonality effects, contamination, peeking or multiple-testing changes and deviations from the plan. Distinguish statistical uncertainty, practical significance and contribution economics. If the data cannot resolve the question, state inconclusive and the evidence that would change it.
